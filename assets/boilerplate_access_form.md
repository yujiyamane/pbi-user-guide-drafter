## Corporate Analytics User Access Form

Access to this Power BI dashboard is managed through the SARA (System Access Request and Approval) portal.

### How to Request Access
1. Navigate to the SARA portal via the NSV Stealth intranet.
2. Search for **"Power BI"** or the relevant application name.
3. Complete the **User Access Request Form**, specifying:
   - Your name and employee number
   - Your manager's name (for approval)
   - The name of the dashboard or Power BI workspace you require access to
   - The level of access required (View only / Contributor / Administrator)
4. Submit the form. Your manager will receive an approval request by email.
5. Once approved, access is provisioned within 2–5 business days.

### Need Help?
Contact the eStealth NSV Service Desk or your local IT support team if you experience issues with access or the SARA portal.
