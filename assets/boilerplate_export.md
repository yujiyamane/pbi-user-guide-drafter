## Export (Excel / PDF)

You can export data from this dashboard in two formats.

### Export to Excel
1. Click on the visual you want to export (e.g. a table or matrix).
2. Click the **More options** button (three dots `...`) in the top-right corner of the visual.
3. Select **Export data**.
4. Choose **Summarised data** (aggregated) or **Underlying data** (raw rows, if permitted).
5. Click **Export** and save the `.xlsx` file.

### Export to PDF
1. Click **File** in the top menu bar.
2. Select **Export to PDF**.
3. The entire report (all visible pages) will be exported as a single PDF document.
4. Save the file to your preferred location.

> **Note:** Export permissions are controlled by the report owner. If export options are greyed out, contact the report owner or your local Power BI administrator.
